# Warzone (MW4) controller settings: menu map and applied values

Observed 2026-11-12, Warzone Season 1 inside the Call of Duty app on Xbox. Menu positions change between seasons: confirm on screen before acting.

## Getting to the settings

1. Launch Call of Duty from the guide; press A at the title screen; press B to close any promo screen.
2. At the game hub (MW4 / Warzone / BO7 tiles), press A on Warzone's PLAY to enter the Warzone lobby. This is the playlist screen, not a match.
3. In the lobby press Menu. A side panel opens on the social tab. Press RB four times to reach the gear tab.
4. Press down once to "Controller", then A.

Traps:

- At the game hub, Menu opens file management with "A Uninstall" on the focused mode. Press B to leave. Never press A there.
- In the lobby, A on a playlist starts matchmaking. Only use Menu, B, and the d-pad there.
- The Controller page reopens on the sub-tab used last.

## Controller page layout

Sub-tabs (LT / RT): Controller, Aiming, Movement, Combat.

Controller tab, top to bottom: Aiming Input Device (locked), Edit Button Layout, Stick Layout Preset, Horizontal Stick Sensitivity, Vertical Stick Sensitivity, Simplified Controls Preset, Low Motor Strain Preset, Bumper Ping, Swap LB/RB with LT/RT, Controller Vibration, Deadzone Inputs.

- Deadzone Inputs expands with X: Test Stick Deadzone, Left Stick Min, Left Stick Max, Right Stick Min, Right Stick Max, Left Trigger, Right Trigger. Step size 1.
- Edit Button Layout opens a sub-screen; the first row is the preset dropdown (Default, Tactical, Lefty, ...).
- Sensitivity sliders step by 0.05.

Aiming tab: Aim Down Sight Behavior, Sensitivity Multiplier (X expands), Look Inversion, Aiming Advanced Settings (A opens), Target Aim Assist, ADS Aim Assist.

- Aiming Advanced Settings: ADS Sensitivity Transition Timing, 3rd Person ADS Correction Type, Aim Response Curve Type, Custom Sensitivity Per Zoom (X expands six rows: Low Zoom, 2x-3x, 4x-5x, 6x-7x, 8x-9x, High Zoom; step 0.05).
- With Custom Sensitivity Per Zoom on, the single ADS Sensitivity Multiplier is locked.

Movement tab: Slide/Dive Behavior, Automatic Airborne Mantle, Sprint Restore, Slide Maintains Sprint, Parachute Automatic Behavior, Movement Advanced Settings, Sprint Assist (X shows Sprint Assist Delay), Mantle Assist, Corner Slice.

- Choosing Tactical Sprint Assist raises a notice: "Switching to Tactical Sprint Assist will make ADS transition timer longer." Relay it; confirm only with the user's approval.
- Movement Advanced Settings: Sprint/Tactical Sprint Behavior, Auto Move Forward, Auto Door Peek, Grounded Mantle, Tactical Sprint Activation, Plunging Underwater, Combat Roll Behavior.

Combat tab: Tactical Stance Behavior, Tactical Stance Activation, Weapon Mount Activation, Dedicated Melee Weapon Activation, Armor Plate Behavior, C4 Detonation Activation, Equipment Behavior, Manual Fire Behavior, Alternate Equipment Throw, Combat Advanced Settings.
